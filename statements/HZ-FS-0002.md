# HZ-FS-0002: The single-inbox merge loses partner mail silently: a filter that misses forwarded sales@ mail, or a reply sent from the Gmail address

**Hazard:** A partner's offer arrives in the operator's Gmail without the label `transport-sales`, and the agent, whose mail scope is that label only, never sees it; or a reply on a partner thread goes out from `evaprual.factorysale@gmail.com` instead of the alias, and the partner's next message lands outside `sales@factory.sale` and its thread.

**Cause:** The forwarder from Radicenter may rewrite the `To:` header, so the filter `to:sales@factory.sale` matches nothing; Gmail replies from the receiving address unless "reply from the same address the message was sent to" is set. Everything in the iteration-1 setup leans on these two settings ([[ADR-FS-0009]], mitigation 2).

**Details:**
- `fs:vault/20-decisions/ADR-0009-separate-transport-email-channel.md@89d77d2`, "Mitigations", item 2: "Verify the forward-through filter actually tags sales@ mail … If it misfires, everything here leans on a broken filter."
- The guide at `89d77d2` has steps 1–4; the filter warning and step 5 (reply from the same address) exist only in the uncommitted edit of 2026-08-27 in the clone ([[DC-FS-0002]]).
- The transfer memo repeats the checklist: forward-with-copy, verify the sent From header, route send-as via Radicenter SMTP, check SPF/DMARC, set "reply from same address" ([[SRC-20260913-memo-transfer#^m20]]).
- The physical setup (forwarder, alias, filters) was still to do on 2026-08-27 and needs Radicenter panel access (HANDOFF); nothing since shows it done ([[FACT-FS-0001]]).

**Consequences:**
- A missed offer: the inquiry waits out its timeout and gets a needless follow-up or a "No Bid"; the offer log and the ranker's dataset are incomplete ([[ADR-FS-0007]]).
- A broken thread: the partner's confirmation reply is not where the operator or the agent looks.
- Send-as through Radicenter SMTP without SPF/DMARC alignment lands partner mail in spam.

**Check:**
- Send a test mail to `sales@factory.sale` from an outside address; confirm it arrives labelled `transport-sales` and read its `Delivered-To` header.
- Reply from Gmail to a partner thread; inspect the `From:` of the sent message and where the partner's answer arrives.
- `dig TXT factory.sale` for SPF; the DMARC record at `_dmarc.factory.sale`; a test to a mailbox that shows authentication results.

**Mitigation:**
- Filter on `deliveredto:sales@factory.sale` (or the partner-domain list) instead of `to:`; label outbound outreach too (`from:me` to a partner).
- Set "reply from the same address the message was sent to"; verify SPF/DMARC for the Radicenter SMTP route.
- Make the verification a step of the test run ([[PLAN-FS-0004]]) before real partner traffic.

**Log:**
- 2026-08-27: named by Juri as mitigation 2 of ADR-0009 and in the guide's uncommitted step 5.
- 2026-10-11: recorded here as a hazard; severity high because the setup is unverified and the agent's whole mail scope depends on it.

**Update 2026-10-11:** the discovery round shows the alias side in use: two of three test mails of 2026-08-27 went out as `sales@factory.sale` from the connected Gmail account, the identity picked by the human in the Gmail UI ([[FIND-FS-0007]], [[FIND-FS-0008]]); the forwarding side and the filter remain unverified (setup item 1, [[SRC-20260916-ops-open-setup-items#^item-1]]). Delamode's and ACE's replies of Deal #7131 arrived "in this mailbox" ([[FIND-FS-0013]]), by which address is not recorded. The hazard stands; severity unchanged.
