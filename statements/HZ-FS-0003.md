# HZ-FS-0003: Partner outreach sent from Bitrix24 leaves no trace in Gmail: the offer log, the agent and the ranker's data miss the partner

**Hazard:** A partner is asked for an offer from Bitrix24 directly; neither the request nor a reply appears in the Gmail account; the offer log, the agent working from the mailbox and the ranker's dataset never see the partner, and "no reply" cannot be told from "never sent".

**Cause:** Bitrix24 sends partner outreach itself, outside the mailbox (Gerava, Deal #7131, 2026-09-09); the Bitrix24–Gmail connection that would mirror it is being worked on ([[SRC-20260916-interview-partner-selection#^q05]]).

**Details:**
- Deal #7131: Gerava was asked from Bitrix24; a search of the whole mailbox, trash and spam included, found nothing to or from Gerava, while Delamode and ACE have complete request-and-reply trails ([[FIND-FS-0012]]).
- Setup item 10 of the discovery round, open, "in progress per operator" ([[SRC-20260916-ops-open-setup-items#^item-10]]).
- The desired-state map makes the mailbox the agent's only view of outreach and names this as its third open design question ([[SRC-20260916-map-desired-state#^n07]]).
- The model: [[ELM-bitrix24]] → transport partner, "invisible in Gmail".

**Consequences:**
- Rows missing from the offer log ([[ELM-drive.offerLog]]): the `chosen` labels and the eval fixtures are biased toward partners that answer by mail.
- Follow-ups by timeout ([[ADR-FS-0004]]) cannot run for a request the agent never saw.
- The 20-cycle count of [[ADR-FS-0016]] is incomplete for any deal with a Bitrix24-side request.

**Check:**
- `rclone cat andrei:vault-ops/operating/open-setup-items.md | grep -n "^## 10"` — is item 10 still open on the shared folder.
- In Bitrix24, the deal's activity stream: outbound mail to partners that has no copy in Gmail (the operator).

**Mitigation:**
- Until the connection exists: the operator logs every partner asked, by any channel, as a row of the offer log with the channel named — the log is the record, not the mailbox.
- Iteration 2: the Bitrix24 webhook ([[ELM-fs.api]]) carries the deal's outbound activities, or all outreach moves to the service and Bitrix24 stops sending.
